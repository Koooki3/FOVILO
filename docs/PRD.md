# Product brief

FOVILO gives users a consistent way to change their viewing setup while preserving the video and its context. The first audience is desktop Chromium users watching regular YouTube videos. A timestamp-link action and immediate return to the original page form the smallest test of an in-context control and recovery.

The MVP hypothesis is that Everyday, Focus and Cinema will make viewing intent easier to express, with the same Context Rail across relevant surfaces and continuity when the route changes. Home, Watch and Search are planned. Compare the native site, Kempt and FOVILO on share-at-time, adjust focus, and resume the previous viewing task. Collect completion, time, mistakes and playback interruptions. This comparison remains open.
